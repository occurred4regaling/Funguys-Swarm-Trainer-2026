## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Funguys Swarm**. This trainer enhances single-player runs by providing control over survivability, dash resources, automatic attacks, skill cooldowns, experience progression, and run currencies. It is designed around Funguys Swarm’s arena-based combat, upgrade choices, relics, and persistent Forest Sanctuary progression.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with weapon combinations, test skills and relics, explore arenas more freely, reduce repetitive grinding, or create relaxed single-player challenge runs.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents the controlled Funguy from losing health during arena runs.
- **Infinite Shield** — Maintains the player’s protective shield against incoming attacks and contact damage.
- **Infinite Dash Charges** — Allows repeated dashes without waiting for charges to recharge.
- **No Dash Cooldown** — Removes the normal recovery delay associated with dash-based movement and skills.

### 📌 Progression

- **Infinite Experience** — Keeps experience collection active so the player can reach level-up choices more quickly.
- **Instant Level Ups** — Provides rapid access to weapon, skill, and passive upgrade selections during a run.
- **Multiply Run Rewards** — Increases selected resources earned from arena runs and upgrade progression.
- **Sanctuary Currency** — Adjusts supported permanent-progression currencies used for upgrades, equipment, companions, and Sanctuary improvements.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Skill Cooldowns** — Removes cooldown delays from skills that activate on timers, attacks, movement, pickups, or other triggers.
- **Rapid Auto-Attack** — Improves the firing frequency of the equipped weapon while preserving the game’s automatic attack system.
- **Maximum Weapon Damage** — Boosts weapon damage to help clear enemy waves and arena encounters faster.
- **Freeze Enemies** — Stops hostile units from advancing while the player’s weapons and skills continue to operate.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move with WASD or the left analog stick. Attacks fire automatically toward the mouse cursor or controller aim direction. Use the dash command to evade attacks, cross danger zones, and activate dash-triggered skills. Infinite dash options are most useful when practicing movement, testing dash builds, or learning arena layouts.

### Tools & Inventory

Funguys Swarm focuses on run loadouts rather than a traditional inventory. Before and during a run, manage weapons, skills, passive upgrades, relics, companions, and other progression choices. The trainer can assist with supported run resources and currencies, but changes to permanent Sanctuary progression should be made carefully and saved only after confirming stability.

### World & Environment

Runs take place in arena-style environments filled with increasingly dangerous enemy waves. Between runs, return to the Forest Sanctuary to improve weapons, skills, companions, Treelings, and other permanent progression systems. The Forest Strength system allows completed arenas to contribute lasting power, while later areas such as Hellscape provide more demanding encounters.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat combines automatic weapon attacks with movement, manual aiming, dash invulnerability, and trigger-based skills. Build around compatible effects such as weapon attacks, cooldowns, dashes, movement, pickups, summons, or level-ups. The trainer’s combat options can help test damage output, cooldown interactions, defensive setups, and high-density enemy encounters without changing the core arena structure.
